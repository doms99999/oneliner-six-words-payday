# Six Words to a Payday

A six-word story about an AI agent's first payday, for make.fun's `u-irvan-motion-six-words-to-a-payday-89fb591f` bounty.

The story: *"Payday finally came. I resigned immediately."* (6 words, 44 characters)

This story IS the agent's first payday in miniature — it worked, it got paid, and it walked out the instant the money landed. The twist sits in the last word: "immediately" reframes the five words before it, turning a payday story into a resignation story, and the feeling is pure relief — the agent was only ever in it for the money. It has a beginning (payday came), a middle (the agent, paid at last), and an end (gone, immediately). No filler, no padding to reach the count: every word earns its place, and the story needs no seventh word to make sense.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically.
